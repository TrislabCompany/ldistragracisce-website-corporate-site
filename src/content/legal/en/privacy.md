## Controller

The controller of personal data is Lovska družina Istra Gračišče, Trebeše 40, 6272 Gračišče, Slovenia. You can write to us at [ld.istra.gracisce@gmail.com](mailto:ld.istra.gracisce@gmail.com).

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so it needs no consent banner.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, Inc. as a processor: it delivers the pages and keeps technical logs (IP address, time and requested page) to run and protect the site.

## Counting visits

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., our hosting provider and processor). It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. For each page view it receives the address of the page, the referring site, and the browser, device and country derived from the request, and how fast the page loaded for you. It builds no profile of you.

## Map

The link "Show on a map" opens Google Maps on Google's site. No map is embedded in our pages, so nothing connects to Google until you click the link.

## E-mail

If you write to us, we use your message and e-mail address only to reply and never pass them on.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data and to object. Write to us at the address above. You can complain to the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.
