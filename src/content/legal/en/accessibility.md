> **Draft:** the club reviews this text before publication.

The hunting club Istra Gračišče wants everyone to be able to use this site. The site follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## How we check it

With every change, each page is checked automatically in a browser, on a wide screen and on a screen 320 pixels wide: no violations found by axe-core, no sideways scrolling, readable text contrast. The site works without JavaScript, the menu opens without it, and every image has a text alternative.

## Known limitations

- Some images are temporary and will be replaced with photos of the hunting ground.
- The documents to download are not published yet; when they are, we will check that they are accessible.

## Tell us about a barrier

If you meet a barrier on this site, write to us at [ld.istra.gracisce@gmail.com](mailto:ld.istra.gracisce@gmail.com). We will reply and remove it.
