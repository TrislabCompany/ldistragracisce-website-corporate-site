title: "Individual red deer hunting"
summary: "Red deer hunting accompanied by one of the club's hunters."
order: 2
---

The guest hunts red deer individually, accompanied by an experienced hunter of the club.
Red deer are our largest game species and live in the wooded parts of the hunting ground.

The season for stags runs from 16 August to 31 December, for hinds from 1 September to 31 December (see the hunting seasons on the Hunting tourism page).
The referent for hunting tourism gives the conditions, dates and price on request.
Guests can stay overnight in the club's hunting lodge.
