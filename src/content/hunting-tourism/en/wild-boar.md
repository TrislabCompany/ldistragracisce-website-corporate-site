---
title: "Group wild boar hunting"
summary: "Group wild boar hunts with the club's hunters."
order: 3
---

Guests join a group wild boar hunt led by the club's hunt leader.

Before a group hunt the club enters every participant and every hunting dog on the list of participants that the Rules on the permit for hunting guests (Pravilnik o dovolilnici za lovske goste) require.
The referent for hunting tourism gives the conditions, dates and price of a group hunt on request.
