# ldistragracisce.si: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied to the product repository as `docs/brief.md` by `handoff-apply`. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

A presentation site for Lovska družina Istra Gračišče, a hunting club in Slovenian Istria: a voluntary, non-profit association that protects, breeds and hunts game and manages its hunting ground. It is rebuilt on `tl-template-website` in the repository `ldistragracisce-website-corporate-site` and replaces today's Angular single-page app, which search engines can't read.

Its readers:

- visiting hunters from abroad, who want the hunting-tourism offers and the seasons, and a way to get in touch;
- club members, who want the documents and the club's contacts;
- people who want to join, who want the requirements and the path to the hunting exam;
- local residents and landowners, who want to report game damage and find the club.

The site informs; it has no sign-in, no stored data and no form. Contact is the club's e-mail link and phone number. Its clearest difference from other Slovenian club sites is the hunting-tourism offer to foreign hunters, in six languages.

## Design direction

The template is only the frame (build, checks, languages, legal pages, hosting). The look is the site's own, in its `src/` (components, layouts, pages, styles). A site that only went through init still looks like the template's sample.

- **Feel:** calm, outdoor and trustworthy: a club that knows and cares for its land. Large photos of the hunting ground (the Karst edge and the Istrian hills, forest and meadow) and of its game, generous white space, few colours. Not a shop and not a trophy gallery. The tone is plain and friendly, written for a visitor who has never been to Istria.
- **Colours:** today's site uses Angular Material defaults plus one own dark green, `#004720`. That green is the starting point; the rest is new. Every text and button pair passes 4.5:1:
  - forest green `#004720` for buttons (white text, 10.9:1), links and headings on the light background (9.9:1);
  - stone white `#f6f3ea` as the page background, ink `#1e2a22` for text (13.4:1), muted `#5b6b5f` for secondary text (5.1:1);
  - earth brown `#8a5a1c` as the accent for small details and a secondary button (white text 5.9:1, on stone white 5.3:1);
  - a dark forest band `#12261a` for the footer and the "Hunting tourism" call, with white text (15.9:1) and a warm ochre `#e9c27a` for its highlights (9.5:1).
- **Type:** a sturdy serif for headings (for example Source Serif 4) and a clear sans for text (for example Inter); both open-licence and self-hosted. No request to Google Fonts.
- **Photos:**
  - shown full-bleed in the home hero and as wide bands between sections, and as cards with the text on a dark gradient;
  - today's site's photos come first; missing ones are stock photos whose licence allows commercial use without attribution;
  - landscape photos show the real hunting ground or Slovenian Istria, never another place; a photo of a game species may be a stock photo;
  - the gallery shows only the club's own photos, which the club supplies;
  - AVIF at a few widths, lazy below the first screen, inside the page-weight budget.
- **Home page, top to bottom:**
  1. the hero: a full-screen photo of the hunting ground, the club's name, one sentence about it, and two buttons ("Hunting tourism", "About the club"); the header lies over it;
  2. the hunting ground in numbers (area, height range, game species, founding year), each with its source;
  3. three ways in as photo cards: Hunting ground and game, Hunting tourism, Hunting lodge;
  4. hunting tourism: the offers in short and the seasons, in the dark band, with a call to get in touch;
  5. for local people: game damage and membership, two short cards;
  6. contact: the club's e-mail link, phone and a Google Maps link;
  7. the footer with the legal pages.
- **Other pages:**
  - About opens with a smaller photo head, then the presentation, the history since the founding, the mission and the organisation by role (no personal names);
  - Hunting ground and game: the area and its terrain, then one section per game species, each with a photo;
  - Hunting tourism: the offers (individual and group hunts, a guide), a table of the hunting seasons per species, what a visiting hunter needs, and how to get in touch;
  - Hunting lodge: photos and what it offers;
  - Game damage: how to report it, step by step, and how to protect fields;
  - Membership: the requirements and the path to the hunting exam;
  - Gallery and Documents: built when the club has supplied their content, and only then in the menu;
  - Contact: the club's e-mail link and phone, the officers by role, the Google Maps link.
- **The site's own components:** the numbers band, the season table (readable on a phone, scrolling inside its own frame), the game-species section, the step list for reporting damage, the document list (title, file type and size, language).
- **Navigation:** Home, About, Hunting ground, Hunting tourism, Hunting lodge, Game damage, Membership, Contact; Gallery and Documents when they have content. On a phone the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript. A language switch in the header with the six languages.
- **From the reference sites:**
  - other Slovenian hunting clubs (ld-rizana.si, ld-izola.si, ld-trnovski-gozd.si): lead with the hunting ground and the game, a section per species, a guide page for becoming a hunter;
  - the Croatian hunting association (ls.hr): hunting tourism and the hunting calendar as first-level items;
  - Kärntner Jägerschaft (kaerntner-jaegerschaft.at): seasons and game species as reference pages, documents to download.
- **Design rules,** checked by `pnpm test` where a machine can:
  - a photo shows at most once on a page;
  - every photo has alternative text in every language; a decorative photo has empty alternative text;
  - landscape photos show the real place; a photo from the earlier site is marked "to check before production" until it is known where it was taken and that the club may publish it;
  - a photo's credit is shown where its licence asks for one; stock photos are chosen only with licences that need none;
  - text on a photo sits on a dark gradient, and the element under it has a dark background colour of its own;
  - no placeholder text (sample text, lorem ipsum, TBD, TODO) reaches any page, in any language, the legal pages included;
  - every page is in its own language, and every language has the same pages and sections;
  - every fact (area, heights, species, years, seasons) cites its source; a fact that can't be checked is left out, and when two sources disagree the detail is left out;
  - every menu link leads to a finished page in the site's design;
  - no `style` attributes in the HTML; long words in headings break at 320px.

## Pages or modules

Several pages, built from the template's sections (`page-sections`), each in six languages:

- **Home:** who the club is and what it offers (see the design direction).
- **About:** the presentation, the history, the mission and the officers by role only.
- **Hunting ground and game:** the area, the terrain and the game species. Today's site gives 6,503 ha, 200–800 m above sea level, eight game species and the founding year 1947; check each fact with the club before it is published.
- **Hunting tourism:** the offers, the seasons, how to get in touch.
- **Hunting lodge:** the lodge and what it offers.
- **Game damage:** the procedure and the contact.
- **Membership:** the requirements and the hunting exam.
- **Gallery** (`photo-gallery`): the club's photos.
- **Documents** (`document-downloads`): the statute, the rules and other club documents as files.
- **Contact** (`contact-without-backend`).
- **Imprint, privacy policy and accessibility statement** (`legal-pages`).

No news page.

## Languages

Slovenian (default, opens first), English, Croatian, Serbian, Italian and German, on every page. Each language has its own URL with `hreflang` and `x-default`, and the sitemap lists every language (`multi-language`). Today's site has sl, en, hr and it, with several pages in Slovenian only; Serbian and German are new. Trislab writes every text in Slovenian and English from today's texts, then translates them; the translations are reviewed before production. Hunting terms (species, seasons) use the terms of each language's hunting associations.

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** ldistragracisce.si. www.ldistragracisce.si redirects to it permanently at the Cloudflare edge.
- **Environments** on Cloudflare Pages: `dev` at dev.ldistragracisce.si (every merge to `main`) and `staging` at staging.ldistragracisce.si (release tags), both behind Cloudflare Access; `production` at production.ldistragracisce.si behind sign-in until the cutover, then at ldistragracisce.si, public. A release is promoted from `staging` after the release approver's sign-off. The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Edge:** ldistragracisce.si is already a zone in Trislab's Cloudflare account (tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/ldistragracisce.si/`). Today the site and the mail are on a cPanel host. At the cutover the apex record to the host goes; the mail records and the mail client set-up records stay, so mail keeps working.
- **Search Console** (`search-console`): the domain property `sc-domain:ldistragracisce.si`, owned by Trislab's role account. Trislab creates it and adds its verification record with the key `google` in tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/ldistragracisce.si/ldistragracisce.si.yaml`, linked in `catalog/domains.yaml`; keep the record through the cutover. The sitemap is `https://ldistragracisce.si/sitemap-index.xml`: Trislab submits it after the cutover and checks the site in Search Console 4–6 weeks later.

## Integrations and outside services

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** cookieless visitor statistics on production only (`web_analytics: true`, the template's `docs/analytics.md`). The privacy policy names it.
- **Google Maps:** a plain link on the Contact page only; nothing is embedded, so nothing loads from Google.
- Everything else is self-hosted (fonts, images, documents). The Content-Security-Policy stays at the template's default plus what analytics needs.

## Quality baseline

The template's whole baseline, no exceptions: `responsive-layout` (mobile first, no sideways scrolling at 320px), `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget`, `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. Uptime alerts come when the platform has them. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page exists in all six languages, with `hreflang`, a canonical address and a sitemap entry; Slovenian opens at the root.
- `ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget, the browser check (no sideways scrolling at 320px, axe-core), the claims check, the design check (no photo twice on a page, no placeholder text) and the `security.txt` check.
- The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from another origin but Cloudflare Web Analytics on production.
- The old routes and www.ldistragracisce.si redirect permanently (301) to the new pages.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the club's structured data.
- SEO (owner: the development team, unless named): the Lighthouse SEO score passes in `ci`; every page has a title, a description and one share image with alt text in all six languages; dev, staging and production.ldistragracisce.si stay behind sign-in and out of search. After the cutover (owner: Trislab): the sitemap is submitted in Search Console, and the old addresses show as redirected, not as errors.
- The design check: the release approver compares staging with this brief's design direction (palette, type, the home page's sections in order, the site's own components); nothing looks like the template's sample.
- Before production: every fact has been checked with the club or its source, the photos from the earlier site are cleared, the translations are reviewed, the legal pages are in place in every language, and the release approver accepts staging.
- After the cutover: mail to the domain still arrives.
